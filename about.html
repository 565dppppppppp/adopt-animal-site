About us

Who we are

Happy Paws began as a neighborhood group caring for lost and injured animals. Today, our volunteers help animals recover and connect them with responsible adopters.

Our mission

We want every animal to be treated with kindness and every adopter to feel prepared for a lasting friendship.

What we do

We provide temporary shelter, basic medical care, food, social time, and adoption guidance for rescued animals.

A short history

Built by neighbors who cared

Our first rescue was a puppy found near a bus stop in 2021. Friends shared food, a spare room, and adoption posts until he found a family. That small success became the beginning of our center.

We now work with local volunteers and families to make rescue a practical, community-supported effort.
